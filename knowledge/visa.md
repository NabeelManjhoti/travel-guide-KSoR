This document is the record's guide to the **visa application process**. The
boundary is the sharpest one in the record:

- **In:** the general *process* of applying for a visa — how the steps
  typically fit together, and the cautions that hold across countries.
- **Out:** exact visa rules, fees, or application deadlines for any specific
  country. The owner confirmed these are not in this record, and a confident
  but wrong deadline is exactly the answer this record refuses to give.

## The one rule to plan everything else around

**Do not make final travel plans or buy tickets until a visa is issued.** The
U.S. visa appointment service states it directly: "Do not make final travel
plans or buy tickets until a visa is issued."[^usvisa-info] A Schengen checklist
from VFS Global says the same thing about round-trip reservations: "Do not
pay for/buy the ticket until a visa has been granted."[^schengen-visa] And the
India process guide ends with the same instruction: "confirm your travel plans
only after obtaining the visa."[^vfs-india-visa] Three different destinations,
one shared rule.

## The universal shape of an application

The clearest statement of the common steps comes from VFS Global's guide to
applying for an Indian visa from the UK, which runs through the whole chain:[^vfs-india-visa]

1. **Purpose of travel and passport** — "Applicant should know their purpose of
   travelling" — tourism, business, employment, student, transit and more —
   "in order to get the right visa application form done", and make sure the
   passport meets the validity/blank-page requirement.
2. **Documents required** — check the visa category's required documents.
3. **Application form** — "After collecting all the necessary documents fill
   your application form online", according to your jurisdiction.
4. **Print and check** — "Make sure all fields are clearly printed"; if you
   find a mistake, "do not overwrite it with pen".
5. **Book an appointment** — submissions "with prior appointments only & as
   per jurisdiction".
6. **Submission** — "Make sure you are bringing your Application form,
   Supporting documents, Appointment Confirmation sheet, Declaration form and
   Correct Visa Fee".
7. **Processing time** — "Processing time vary from application to application"
   — "confirm your travel plans only after obtaining the visa".
8. **Tracking** — "Track your application online, or by automated SMS/EMAIL".
9. **Collection** — counter collection or courier.

Canada's visitor-visa steps compress the same chain: "Step 1: Get the
documents that show you meet the criteria", "Step 2: Apply online" — create an
account, "Upload your documents", "Pay your fees" — "Step 3: Give your
fingerprints and photo (biometrics)", and afterwards "If we approve your
application, we'll ask you to send us your passport."[^canada-visa]

## Examples of what countries actually add on top

These show the *kinds* of extra steps — never the rules to rely on. Check the
specific destination before acting.

**China** (via a Chinese visa application service centre): prepare documents;
complete the online form **and upload** the application documents for a
preliminary review; then visit the centre to submit originals and pay, where
"all applicants holding ordinary passports will be requested to have their
fingerprints collected"; then collect your passport.[^china-visa]

**United States** (visitor/business): complete the DS-160 online form first —
"Each applicant must complete this form before using any services on this
website" — then return to the appointment service to schedule a Consular
Section appointment; consular officers "reserve the right to require an
interview for any visa applicant".[^usvisa-info]

**Schengen area** (example checklist, Norway visa for Philippines applicants):
"Original passport must have at least 2 unused pages" and "should also be
valid for at least 3 months after planned departure from the Schengen Area"; a
passport photo not older than 6 months; travel medical insurance whose "minimum
coverage shall be EUR 30,000"; and a round-trip **reservation** — "Do not pay
for/buy the ticket until a visa has been granted."[^schengen-visa]

## Biometrics and interviews are the commonest additions

Both named countries above add a physical step: Canada's "your fingerprints and
photo (biometrics)"[^canada-visa] and China's fingerprint collection at the
centre.[^china-visa] The US example shows the other common one — an interview
at the consular section.[^usvisa-info] Which one applies, and to whom, is
always per-country — exactly what this record declines to state by heart.
