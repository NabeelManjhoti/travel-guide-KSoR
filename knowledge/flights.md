This document is the record's guide to cheap flights. The owner named "cheap
flights" as core to the record in the intake interview.[^intake-interview-2026-09-14]
Two boundaries are deliberate:

- **Cheap** flights. Business-class and premium-deal guidance is out of scope.
- The record points at **how to find** cheap flights — it does not book or buy
  tickets on anyone's behalf.

## What is covered

- The workflow and approach for finding cheap flights.

Open questions:

- What is the owner's actual workflow — when to book, which comparison tools,
  budget airlines, flexible-date approaches, anything that makes one fare
  cheap and another not?
- Which routes should the record cover first?

Until the owner answers these, this document cannot name a method or a fare
rule — and it won't.
