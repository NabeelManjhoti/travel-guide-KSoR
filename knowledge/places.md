This document is the record's guide to the best places to visit on a budget.
The owner named "best places to visit, budget friendly" as core to the record
in the intake interview.[^intake-interview-2026-09-14] The budget positioning is
the whole point: a destination that needs a luxury budget does not belong here.

## What is covered

- Destinations that are worth visiting without spending much.
- Roughly what makes each one worth it and what to see there.

Open questions:

- Which specific destinations does the owner want to cover first?
- What budget band counts as "budget friendly" — per person, per day, or per trip?
- For each destination: what to see, best season, and a rough daily budget?

Until the owner answers these, this document cannot name any one place, and it
won't — a destination invented here would be a claim nothing governs.

[^intake-interview-2026-09-14]: Owner's words in the intake interview; the boundary around budget-friendly places was confirmed there.
